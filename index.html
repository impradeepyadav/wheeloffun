Spin the Wheel — Free Random Name Picker & Decision Maker

Can't decide? Let the wheel do it. Add names, foods, movies, or yes/no options and spin for an instant, truly random result. Free, private, and works on any device — no account required.

What Is the Spin the Wheel Tool?

A spin the wheel tool turns a hard decision into a fun, fair, one-second reveal. Add any list — names, foods, answers, tasks, movie titles — then spin and let crypto.getRandomValues pick the winner. No bias, no argument, no "you always pick the same person."

People actually use it for:

  • Giveaway & raffle winners on livestreams, Discords, and classrooms
  • Who goes first in board games, sports drills, or group projects
  • What to eat when nobody in the group chat has an opinion
  • Yes / No decisions — or "Maybe," "Definitely," "Ask again"
  • Picking a student to answer (fairly — everyone can see the spin)
  • Assigning chores, meeting topics, movie-night picks, workout moves
  • Team splits — Red vs Blue, Alpha vs Beta

If you need a fast, transparent, tamper-proof random pick, the wheel is the tool.

Random Name Picker — Spin to Pick a Name

Paste any list of names — one per line — and spin. The wheel updates live as you type, so you can add or remove names without reloading. Every name gets an equal chance on every spin.

Perfect for teachers calling on students, hosts picking chat members, team leads assigning tasks, and friends deciding who pays the bill. Use remove-winner mode to avoid picking the same person twice across multiple rounds.

Have 40 students and one slot left? The wheel handles up to 50 entries, and supports photos or emojis instead of text if you want the pick to be instantly recognizable on stream.

What to Eat Wheel — Finally Decide Dinner

Open the fridge. Stare into it. Close it. Spin the wheel instead. Add your usual suspects — pizza, sushi, tacos, ramen, that one place you keep forgetting about — and let the wheel pick breakfast, lunch, dinner, or a late-night snack.

Customize it for quick lunches, weekend treats, restaurant delivery options, or meal-plan roulette. Delete anything you don't want tonight, spin once, and stop scrolling food apps.

Keep only foods you'd actually eat right now — a wheel full of leftovers won't taste any better.

Yes or No Wheel — One-Spin Answers

Want more nuance? Add "Maybe," "Definitely," "Ask again later," or "Not now" to keep the energy playful. Just remember: the wheel is random, so use it for casual or lighthearted decisions — not for things that deserve careful thought.

Is the Wheel Truly Random?

Yes — and it's worth explaining why, so you can trust the result.

The wheel uses crypto.getRandomValues, a browser API that produces cryptographically secure random numbers — the same class of randomness password managers use. It's not Math.random(), and it doesn't "even out" or favor certain segments.

Every segment on the wheel has an equal probability of winning on any spin. If you have 10 names, each has a 1 in 10 chance. No weighting, no hidden bias, no algorithm that "remembers" past winners.

The spin animation is purely visual. The winner is chosen the moment you click SPIN, before the wheel starts moving. The animation is just there to make it dramatic — the result is already decided.

How to Use the Wheel Spinner

  1. Add your entries. Type or paste one item per line into the text box. The wheel redraws live as you type.
  2. Customize (optional). Open Settings to change colors, spin duration, sound, winner popup text, and more. Defaults work great — nothing is required.
  3. Spin it. Click SPIN, click anywhere on the wheel, or press Ctrl + Enter. All three do the same thing.
  4. See the winner. A popup shows the result. Close it to spin again, or click Remove to drop the winner from the list and spin for the next round.

That's the whole flow. Nothing else to learn.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use the random name picker?

Paste your names into the text box, one per line. Then click SPIN, click the wheel, or press Ctrl + Enter. The wheel picks a random winner and shows it in a popup.

How do I make a Yes or No wheel?

Type Yes and No as the two entries (one per line), then spin. You can also add more options like "Maybe," "Definitely," or "Ask again later."

Can I use the wheel for a giveaway or raffle?

Absolutely. Add every participant's name, enable remove-winner mode for multiple rounds, and use fullscreen mode so your audience can see the spin live. The result is verifiably random thanks to crypto.getRandomValues.

Can I use the wheel to decide what to eat?

Yes — it's one of the most popular uses. Add meals, restaurants, or cuisines, spin once, and commit to the result. No more group-chat "you pick" loops.

Can I upload images instead of names?

Yes. Click the image icon in the toolbar to upload a photo — it will be downscaled and displayed on the wheel segment. The filename becomes the entry name, so you can still edit it later.

How many entries can the wheel hold?

Up to 50 entries. Fewer entries are easier to read, especially on mobile or stream — 10–15 is the sweet spot for most uses.

Is the wheel free to use?

Yes. No account, no subscription, no paywall. All features are included.

Does the wheel work on mobile?

Yes. The layout is fully responsive and the wheel supports touch, tap, and swipe. Works on phones, tablets, laptops, and desktops with any modern browser.

What happens if I close the tab?

Unsaved entries are lost — everything lives in your browser session. To keep a wheel for later, use File → Save Wheel, or Export it as a .json file.

Can I save my wheel and come back to it?

Yes. Use File → Save Wheel to store up to 20 wheels in your browser. Open File → Load Wheel to bring one back. You can also export a .json file for backup or to share with a friend.

Is the spin result really random?

Yes — it uses crypto.getRandomValues, which produces cryptographically secure random numbers. Every segment has an equal chance on every spin, and the winner is chosen before the wheel starts animating.

Can I customize how the wheel looks?

Yes. Open Settings to choose from 34 color themes or add your own custom colors, toggle segment borders, show/hide the pointer and text, set the spin duration, customize the winner popup message, and control sound and music.

Is there a keyboard shortcut to spin?

Yes. Press Ctrl + Enter (Windows / Linux) or ⌘ + Enter (Mac) to spin without using the mouse.
