Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about wheeloffun.app? We've got detailed answers. Everything you need to know about using our spinner tool.

How does the wheeloffun.app spinner work?

Simply go to wheeloffun.app, type or paste your options (one per line) into the text box, then click the SPIN button. The wheel updates instantly as you type. You can also click anywhere on the wheel or press Ctrl+Enter to spin. The wheel slows down to reveal a random winner in a popup, where you can choose to close it or remove the winner for the next round.

Is the wheel on wheeloffun.app really random?

Yes, it is 100% mathematically random. We use the browser's built-in crypto.getRandomValues function, which generates cryptographically secure random numbers. This is the same technology used by password managers. The winner is mathematically chosen before the wheel even starts spinning, ensuring no bias or hidden algorithms are involved.

How many entries can I add to the wheel?

You can add up to 50 entries per wheel. However, for the best visual experience, we highly recommend keeping the list to 15 items or fewer. If you have too many entries, the text inside each slice becomes very small and hard to read. If you exceed 50, the system will automatically trim the extra lines.

Can I use the wheel to pick multiple winners?

Yes, absolutely. If you are doing a raffle for 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place, simply enable the 'Remove Winner' mode in the settings. After each spin, the winning name is automatically deleted from the wheel, guaranteeing they cannot win again and ensuring every winner is unique.

Does wheeloffun.app work on mobile phones and tablets?

Yes, the tool is fully responsive. On mobile devices, the layout automatically adjusts, moving the text entry box below the wheel for easier typing. The spinning animation, sounds, and all features work perfectly on iOS (iPhone/iPad) and Android devices using any modern browser.

Can I customize the colors and speed of the wheel?

Yes, there are extensive customization options. Open the Settings menu (gear icon) on wheeloffun.app to choose from over 30 color themes (like Neon, Ocean, or Galaxy), set custom colors, adjust the spin duration from a quick 1.5 seconds to a dramatic 15 seconds, and control the sound volume.

Is my data private and saved on your servers?

Your privacy is our top priority. All data processing happens entirely locally in your browser—your entries are never sent to or saved on wheeloffun.app's servers. If you use the 'Save Wheel' feature, it is stored in your browser's local storage. You can also export your wheels as JSON files to keep a backup on your own computer.

Is wheeloffun.app free to use?

Yes, wheeloffun.app is completely free. There are no hidden charges, premium tiers, or subscriptions. You don't even need to create an account or log in. You can use all features, including custom colors, image uploads, and full-screen mode, at zero cost.

Can I save my wheel for later?

Yes. Use the 'File' menu on wheeloffun.app to save your current wheel. It will be stored in your browser's local storage. You can load it anytime using the 'Load Wheel' option. You can also export the wheel as a .json file to share it with a friend or move it to another device.

Does the wheel work offline?

Once the page is fully loaded in your browser, the spinning functionality continues to work without an internet connection. Because all the logic and animations run locally in your browser, you don't need to stay connected to spin the wheel.

Can I add images or emojis to the wheel?

Yes! You can paste emojis directly into the text box alongside your text. You can also use the image icon to upload photos. The tool will automatically resize and crop your images to fit perfectly into the wheel's slices, adding a fun visual element to your game or giveaway.

How do I remove a winner after spinning?

When the spin ends, a popup window displays the winner. You have two options: click 'Close' to keep them on the wheel, or click 'Remove' to delete them. Alternatively, you can go to Settings and turn on 'Auto-remove winner' so the winning entry disappears automatically every time.

Can I change the language of the tool?

Yes. The interface is fully translated into multiple languages including English, Spanish, Indonesian, Portuguese, German, and French. You can switch languages using the globe icon in the navigation menu.

What is the "Yes or No" wheel used for?

The 'Yes or No' wheel is a preset option designed to help you make quick, low-stakes decisions. It's perfect for things like deciding what to eat, whether to buy a small item, or settling a friendly debate. You can even add options like 'Maybe' or 'Definitely' for more variety.

Are there sound effects during the spin?

Yes. The tool features satisfying ticking sounds as the wheel spins, a celebratory chime when a winner is picked, and optional background music. You can adjust the volume between 0% and 100%, or mute everything entirely using the speaker icon or the Settings menu.

How do I clear all my entries quickly?

To wipe the wheel clean instantly, go to the 'New Wheel' menu in the top navigation and select 'Blank Wheel'. This will instantly clear all entries, reset the colors to default, and start you with a fresh slate.

Can I share the winner with others?

Yes. When the winner is announced, there is a 'Share' button. Clicking this will generate a clean text summary of the result. You can copy this text and paste it into WhatsApp, Discord, or any other messaging app to show your friends.

Why does the text sometimes get cut off on the wheel?

The wheel is designed to be visually balanced. If you enter very long sentences, the tool automatically truncates (shortens) the text so it fits neatly inside the wedge without overlapping. We recommend using short names, numbers, or single words for the best visual experience.

Is there a dark mode?

The main tool automatically adapts to your system's dark or light mode preference if supported by your browser. This ensures the interface is comfortable to look at, whether you are working in a bright room or late at night.

What browsers are supported?

Wheel of Fun is built with modern web standards. It works perfectly on Google Chrome, Apple Safari, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, and Brave, across both desktop computers and mobile devices (iOS and Android). Ensure JavaScript is enabled in your browser.
