Random Name Picker: Pick Winners Fairly Every Time

By Wheel of Fun Team · Updated on: Oct 10, 2023 · 4 min read

If you've ever hosted a giveaway on Instagram, tried to pick a student to answer a question in class, or had to decide which family member gets the last piece of cake, you know how tricky it can be. People get suspicious if you pick the same person twice, and let's be honest—humans are naturally biased, even if we don't mean to be.

That's exactly why a Random Name Picker is such a lifesaver. At Wheel of Fun, we've built a tool that completely removes the bias from the equation. You just put the names in, spin the wheel, and let the math do the rest.

Why Use a Random Name Picker?

Whether you're running a business, a classroom, or a gaming stream, fairness is key. Here is why our tool is the best way to pick a winner:

  • 100% Unbiased: The tool doesn't know if someone is your best friend or a complete stranger. It uses a secure random number generator, so everyone has an equal chance.
  • Transparency: When you spin a wheel in front of an audience (live or on video), everyone can see the process. There are no hidden tricks.
  • Excitement: It's way more fun to watch a colorful wheel spin than to watch someone pull a name out of a hat.

How to Use the Random Name Picker (Step-by-Step)

Setting up your name picker takes less than a minute. Here's how:

  1. Open the Tool: Go to wheeloffun.app on your browser.
  2. Enter the Names: In the text box, paste your list of names. Make sure there is one name per line. You can add up to 50 names at a time.
  3. Customize (Optional): Want to make it look cool? Go to the settings and change the color theme or turn on the sound effects for extra suspense.
  4. Spin: Hit the big "SPIN" button. Once the wheel stops, a popup will reveal your random winner!

Pro Tip: The "Remove Winner" Feature

If you're picking multiple winners (like a 1st, 2nd, and 3rd prize), you don't want the same person winning twice. In the settings menu, toggle on "Remove Winner". After the wheel stops, that person's name will be automatically removed from the list before your next spin. This guarantees that every winner is unique.

Creative Ways to Use It

People use the name picker for a lot more than just giveaways. Here are some of our favorite use cases:

  • Teachers: Add all your students' names to randomly call on them during a lesson. It keeps everyone on their toes!
  • Streamers: Paste the usernames of everyone who entered your chat giveaway and spin live on stream.
  • Office Teams: Use it to decide who has to take meeting notes or who gets to leave early on a Friday.
  • Board Games: If a game doesn't specify who goes first, let the wheel decide.

Stop dealing with "that's not fair!" complaints. Let the wheel handle the tough decisions with complete transparency.
