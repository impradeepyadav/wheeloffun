How to Use Wheel of Fun: The Ultimate Beginner's Guide

By Wheel of Fun Team · Updated on: Oct 20, 2023 · 5 min read

Have you ever been stuck deciding where to eat with friends, or struggled to pick a fair winner for a giveaway? The Wheel of Fun is a free, online spinner tool designed to solve these exact problems. Whether you're a teacher, a streamer, or just someone who hates making decisions, spinning the wheel adds a layer of fairness and excitement to any choice.

In this guide, we will walk you through exactly how to use the tool, customize your wheels, and explore all the interactive features available at wheeloffun.app.

Step 1: Add Your Entries

The core of the tool is the wheel itself, and you get to control what goes on it. When you open the website, you will see a text box on the right side (or at the bottom if you are on mobile). This is where you add your options.

  • Type or Paste: Simply type your options, pressing "Enter" after each one. You can paste a whole list of names from an Excel sheet or a document.
  • One per Line: Ensure each option is on its own line. The wheel automatically updates as you type, giving you instant visual feedback.
  • Limits: You can add up to 50 entries per wheel. However, for the best visual experience, it's recommended to keep the list shorter (around 10-15 items) so the text remains readable on the segments.

Step 2: Spin the Wheel

Once your options are ready, it's time for the fun part. You have three easy ways to trigger a spin:

  1. Click the big "SPIN" button located in the center of the wheel.
  2. Click anywhere on the wheel itself.
  3. Use the keyboard shortcut: Ctrl + Enter (or Cmd + Enter on Mac).

The wheel will spin for a few seconds and gradually slow down to reveal your random winner. A popup will appear showing the result. Just close the popup to spin again.

Step 3: Customize Your Wheel (Optional)

While the default settings work perfectly, Wheel of Fun offers powerful customization options to make your wheel unique. Click on the Settings (gear icon) to explore:

  • Spin Speed: Adjust the duration from a quick 1.5 seconds to a dramatic 15-second spin.
  • Themes & Colors: Choose from over 30 color themes like Neon, Ocean, or Galaxy, or create your own custom color palette.
  • Sound & Confetti: Turn on the "tick" sounds as the wheel spins, and control the volume of the winner chimes. You can even toggle the celebratory confetti on or off.
  • Image Entries: Instead of just text, you can upload photos to appear on the wheel segments.

Pro Tip: Use "Remove Winner" Mode

If you are doing multiple rounds (like picking winners for 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place), you don't want the same person to win twice. Toggle on the "Remove Winner" mode in the settings. After a spin, the winning entry will be automatically removed from the wheel before your next spin, ensuring a fair selection every time.

What Can You Use Wheel of Fun For?

The tool is incredibly versatile. Here are just a few ways people use it daily:

  • Random Name Picker: Teachers can call on students fairly, or streamers can pick a random viewer for a giveaway.
  • Yes or No Wheel: Just add "Yes" and "No" to quickly settle a lighthearted debate.
  • What to Eat: Add your favorite restaurants or meals and let the wheel decide your dinner plans.
  • Chores & Tasks: Assign household chores to family members in a fun way.

Is It Really Random?

Yes! Wheel of Fun uses the browser's built-in crypto.getRandomValues function to ensure cryptographically secure randomness. This means every single entry has an equal chance of being picked. There is no hidden algorithm or weighting—the result is 100% fair.
