Spin the Wheel: Fun Ways to Use the Tool Daily

By Wheel of Fun Team · Updated on: Oct 05, 2023 · 4 min read

When most people hear "spin the wheel," they immediately think of game shows or carnivals. But what if we told you that a digital spinning wheel could actually make your everyday life easier, more organized, and a lot more fun?

At Wheel of Fun, our tool is designed to be more than just a randomizer. It's a way to break decision fatigue and add a little spark of excitement to your daily routine. Here are some of our favorite, creative ways to use the wheel every single day.

1. The "Dinner Dilemma" Solver

"What should we eat?" is probably the most common question in any household. Instead of arguing for 30 minutes, open the wheel. Add options like "Pizza," "Tacos," "Pasta," "Salad," and "Order In." Give it a spin, and voila! The decision is made, and you can finally start cooking (or ordering).

2. Workout Roulette

Tired of doing the same boring exercises? Create a "Workout Roulette" wheel. Add different exercises like:

  • 20 Pushups
  • 30 Jumping Jacks
  • 1-Minute Plank
  • 15 Squats
  • Rest for 1 Minute (everyone loves this slice!)

Spin it 5 times for a quick, randomized full-body workout.

3. Chore Buster for Families

Nobody likes doing chores, but spinning a wheel makes it feel like a game. Add all the household chores that need to be done (washing dishes, taking out the trash, vacuuming, folding laundry) and have each family member spin the wheel to see what their task is for the day. It stops the "I always do everything!" arguments.

4. Truth or Dare (Family-Friendly)

Having a game night? Use the wheel to decide the fate of the players. Create a wheel with "Truth" and "Dare" options, or get specific. You can type in fun, harmless dares like "Speak in an accent for the next round" or "Do your best celebrity impression."

5. TV Show or Movie Night

Spending 40 minutes browsing Netflix and then deciding to just rewatch "The Office" again? We've all been there. Before you sit on the couch, put 4-5 movies or genres on the wheel. Whatever it lands on, that's what you watch. No take-backs!

Make It Your Own

The best part about the wheel is that it’s completely customizable. With our advanced settings, you can change the colors to match your mood, adjust the spin speed for dramatic effect, and even turn on the sound effects for that satisfying "tick-tick-tick" as it slows down.

Ready to make your day a little more random? Grab your phone or laptop and give it a spin!
