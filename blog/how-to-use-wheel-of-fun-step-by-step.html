Wheel of Fun Guide: How to Use It Step by Step

By Wheel of Fun Team · Nov 09, 2026 · 4 min read

If you are visiting Wheel of Fun for the first time, you might be wondering, "How do I actually use this thing?" Don't worry—it is incredibly easy. Whether you are a teacher picking a student, a streamer hosting a giveaway, or just someone who can't decide what to eat, this guide will walk you through everything you need to know.

Step 1: Enter Your Options

When you open the main page, you will see a large wheel in the center and a text box on the right side (or bottom on mobile). This is where the magic happens.

  • Type or Paste: Click inside the text box and start typing. You can paste a whole list of names directly from Excel or a document.
  • One Per Line: Make sure each option is on a new line. As you type, you will see the wheel update instantly with your entries and colors.

Step 2: Spin the Wheel

Once your list is ready, it's time to spin. You have three easy ways to do this:

  1. Click the big SPIN button right in the middle of the wheel.
  2. Click anywhere on the wheel itself.
  3. Use your keyboard and press Ctrl + Enter (or Cmd + Enter on Mac).

The wheel will spin with a satisfying tick sound and slow down to reveal your random winner in a popup window.

Step 3: Make It Your Own (Optional)

The default settings are great, but you can customize the wheel to fit your vibe. Click the Settings (gear icon) to explore:

  • Themes: Choose from 30+ color palettes like Neon, Ocean, or Pastel.
  • Spin Speed: Make it super fast (1.5s) or dramatic and slow (15s).
  • Sound: Adjust the volume of the ticking sounds and the winning chime.
  • Images: Instead of text, you can even upload small images to appear on the wheel segments.

Step 4: Managing Your Results

When the wheel stops, a popup shows the winner. From here, you have two choices:

  • Close: Just close the popup to keep everyone on the wheel for the next spin.
  • Remove Winner: If you are doing multiple rounds (like 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place), click "Remove" to take the winner off the wheel so they can't win twice.
